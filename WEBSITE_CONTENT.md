## The series
Every Gesond night belongs to a series, so you always know the kind of night you're walking into.
- Saturday Linkup (flagship): A full line-up of local DJs takes turns on the decks from early evening until late, with an MC keeping the floor moving. Ten editions in and still growing. Lekompo, Amapiano.
- Gesond Presents: Headline sets and live performances from guest DJs and artists, announced with their own posters.
- Lekompo Vibes: The sound of Limpopo, played loud, by DJs who know it best.
- Afro House Back to Back: DJs trade sets back to back for a deeper, longer groove.
- Seasonal sessions: One-off nights like the End of Winter Session, Saturday Explosion and Bhazuka Boomnation.

## However you like your night
Gesond is for the whole community, whether you're 18 or 58.
- Here for the decks: Come for the line-up. Get there before the headline set, find your spot near the DJ and stay until the last track.

- Here to celebrate: Birthdays, reunions and team outings. Tell us your date and group size and we'll help plan food and drinks for your table.

## Good to know before you come
- 18 and over only. Bring your ID. We don't sell alcohol to anyone under 18.
- Entry. Most sessions are free entry. The poster for each event confirms entry and cooler-box charges, usually R50 for a cooler box.
- Respect. Look after each other and our staff. We may refuse entry or service to keep the night safe.
- Get home safe. Drink responsibly and arrange your lift before the night starts. Don't drink and drive.
