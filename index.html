Food and drinks

Eat before the floor fills up, or order to the table all night.

From the kitchen

  • From the fire: flame-grilled meats and braai favourites, cooked to order.
  • From the pot: slow-cooked, home-style African dishes.
  • From home: pap, sides, starters and comfort food.

Dine in or take away. Ask for today's menu and specials, and tell us about allergies or dietary needs before you order.

From the bar

  • Beers and lagers: Castle Lager, Castle Lite, Black Label, Heineken, Windhoek, Stella Artois
  • Ciders and coolers: Hunter's, Savanna, Brutal Fruit, Strongbow, Flying Fish, Bernini
  • Spirits: Johnnie Walker, Hennessy, Klipdrift, Richelieu, Gordon's, Red Square, J&B
  • Wine, bubbles and soft drinks, including Coca-Cola, Schweppes and Red Bull

Stock changes with availability. Brand names belong to their owners.

Good to know before you come

  • 18 and over only. Bring your ID. We don't sell alcohol to anyone under 18.
  • Entry. Most sessions are free entry. The poster for each event confirms entry and cooler-box charges, usually R50 for a cooler box.
  • Respect. Look after each other and our staff. We may refuse entry or service to keep the night safe.
  • Get home safe. Drink responsibly and arrange your lift before the night starts. Don't drink and drive.
